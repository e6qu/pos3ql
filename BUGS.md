# Blocked defects

No known defects are blocked by an external dependency or an intractable problem.

Fix reproducible defects in the PR that finds them, including incidental defects.
A failing check, reproducible mismatch or unfinished qualification belongs to the active PR and
[PLAN.md](PLAN.md), not this list. Record a defect here only with its reproducer,
observed behavior and exact external blocker or reason it cannot be repaired.
Keep resolved investigations with their fixing code, tests and reference evidence.

[CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) owns verification and failure diagnostics;
current behavior and limits belong in the [owning guides](docs/README.md).
